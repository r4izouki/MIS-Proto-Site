About Saudi Aramco

Saudi Aramco is one of the world's largest oil and gas companies and a global leader in energy and petrochemicals. Headquartered in Dhahran, Aramco operates in over 50 countries and employs over 70,000 people worldwide. For students, Aramco offers one of the most respected internship programs in the region — with hands-on project experience, mentorship from industry experts, and a strong pathway to full-time employment.
