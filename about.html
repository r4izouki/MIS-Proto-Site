About the SIP Platform

Student Internship Program — Prince Mohammad Bin Fahd University

What is SIP?

The Student Internship Program (SIP) is an initiative developed at Prince Mohammad Bin Fahd University to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world professional experience. Our platform connects PMU students with leading companies across the Eastern Province and beyond, making the internship search process easier, faster, and more transparent for everyone involved.

Our Mission

We believe every student deserves a fair opportunity to launch their career. SIP was built to eliminate the frustration of cold-emailing companies, chasing down HR departments, and never hearing back. Through our platform, companies post their internship requirements directly, and students can apply with a single profile — no repeated paperwork, no confusion.

How It Works

1

Create your student profile — sign up with your university email, enter your major, GPA, and basic details.

2

Browse companies — explore verified companies that are actively looking for interns in your field.

3

Apply directly — submit your application through the platform. Companies review your profile and respond through the system.

4

Track your applications — check your account page at any time to see the status of your submissions.
